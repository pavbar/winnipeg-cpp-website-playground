## Winnipeg C++ Developers - February 2026

![February 2026 Meetup](/images/event.jpeg)

Mark your calendars! Our February meetup is scheduled and we'd love to see you there.

### Event Details

**Date:** Thursday, February 26, 2026  
**Time:** 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM CST  
**Location:** Bill and Helen Norrie Library  
15 Poseidon Bay, Winnipeg, MB R3M 3E4

### What to Expect

Welcome to Winnipeg C++ Developers!

We are doing it again and talking all about C++ STL algorithms. Some you may know, and some you may not know yet.

This is open to anyone interested in the C++ programming language, whether you work with it professionally or as a
hobby. We will begin on topic, but we are happy to diverge, even to programming languages other than C++.

If this piques your interest, please join us!
