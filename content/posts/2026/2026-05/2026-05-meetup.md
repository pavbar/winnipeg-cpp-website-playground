## Winnipeg C++ Developers - May 2026

![May 2026 Meetup](/images/event.jpeg)

Mark your calendars! Our May meetup is scheduled and we'd love to see you there.

### Event Details

**Date:** Thursday, May 28, 2026  
**Time:** 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM CDT  
**Location:** Bill and Helen Norrie Library  
15 Poseidon Bay, Winnipeg, MB R3M 3E4

### What to Expect

May 2026 event for the Winnipeg C++ Developers user group.

Welcome to Winnipeg C++ Developers!

This month, we have a guest speaker!! Max Hughson will be presenting on east vs west const and on API design
for passing bools to an interface.

This is open to those who are interested in the C++ programming language, whether you work with it professionally
or as a hobby. We will begin on topic, but we will be free to diverge, even to programming languages other than C++.

If this piques your interest, then please join us!
